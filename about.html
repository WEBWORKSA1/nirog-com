About Nirog.com

Nirog (निरोग) means "free of disease". We believe most Indian families could avoid years of illness and lakhs in hospital bills by catching problems early: a blood-sugar test at 35, a blood pressure check at the pharmacy, a waist that stays under half your height, and health insurance that actually covers what it should.

What we do

  • Free tools such as BMI with Asian cut-offs, the Indian Diabetes Risk Score and a health insurance cover calculator.
  • Plain-English guides on prevention, common conditions, nutrition, sleep, movement and health insurance, each with listed sources.
  • Free advice: we connect readers who ask for help with licensed insurance advisors, accredited labs and registered doctors.
  • Community: contests, awareness campaigns and check-up camps funded by supporters and sponsors.

How we make money

We are funded by advertising (Google AdSense), referral fees from vetted partners when a reader asks to be connected, sponsorships that are always labelled, and voluntary reader support. Money never buys a recommendation or changes the facts in a guide. Read more in our editorial policy.

What we are not

We are not a hospital, clinic, pharmacy or insurance company, and we do not diagnose or treat anyone. Always consult a qualified professional about your own health. In an emergency call 108 or 112.
