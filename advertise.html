Our partner standards

  • Health claims must be lawful and evidence-based; no "cure" claims.
  • Insurance partners must be IRDAI-licensed; labs NABL-accredited or equivalent; doctors registered with NMC or a State Medical Council.
  • Sponsored content is always labelled.
  • Leads are shared only with the consent of the person, for the purpose they asked for.
