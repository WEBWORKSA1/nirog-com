Common questions

What does “nirog” mean?

Nirog is a Hindi and Sanskrit word meaning free of disease, or healthy. That is our goal for every reader: prevention first, so fewer people need hospital care.

Is Nirog.com free?

Yes. All guides, tools and the Nirog Score are free. We are funded by advertising, reader support and partner referral fees, which never change what you pay.

Can Nirog.com diagnose my condition?

No. We provide general, evidence-based information and tools. Only a qualified doctor who examines you can diagnose and treat a condition.

How do you choose what to publish?

We write from authoritative sources such as WHO, ICMR, MoHFW, NHS and peer-reviewed research, list every source, and update guides when guidance changes. Read our editorial policy.
