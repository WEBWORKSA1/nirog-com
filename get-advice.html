Questions people ask

Is the advice really free?

Yes. You pay nothing for advice. If you choose to buy a policy or book a test through a partner, the partner may pay us a referral fee; it never changes the price you pay.

Who will contact me?

A Nirog advisor or a vetted partner: an IRDAI-licensed insurance intermediary for insurance requests, or a NABL-accredited lab or registered clinic for check-ups. We share your details only for the request you make.

Will I get spam calls?

No. We contact you only about your request, at the time you choose, and you can opt out at any time. We never sell your data to random call centres.

Do you recommend one insurer over another?

We compare features such as room-rent limits, co-pay, waiting periods and network hospitals against your needs. We don't push a single company.

Nirog.com is an information and referral platform. We are not an insurer or a medical provider. Insurance is solicited only by IRDAI-licensed partners; read the policy wording before you buy.
